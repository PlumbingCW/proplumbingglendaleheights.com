When a toilet problem is an emergency

An overflowing toilet that will not stop, a toilet leaking at the base into the ceiling below and a toilet that backs up along with other drains are all urgent. A single clogged bowl is something you can try to plunge first. If the water rises on a second flush, stop and call.

If several drains are slow, the problem is likely farther down the line. Our drain cleaning team handles that case. For a single fixture, we repair or replace the toilet itself.

Many repairs cost far less than a new toilet, and we tell you when a repair is worth doing. Parts such as a flapper or a fill valve are quick to swap. A cracked bowl or a repeatedly failing older unit is a replacement candidate.

Leaks at the base

A toilet that leaks at the base is not just wet flooring. Water seeps into the subfloor and can rot it, so it should be fixed quickly. The usual cause is a worn wax ring, and the fix is to pull the toilet, replace the ring, inspect the flange and set it again.

If the flange is cracked or corroded, we repair it before resetting. A toilet that rocks even slightly breaks the seal again, so we make sure it sits flat and the bolts are snug without overtightening.

Installing a new toilet

A new toilet is a straightforward upgrade when the old unit is cracked, stained beyond cleaning or wasteful. Choose between one-piece and two-piece designs, round or elongated bowls and standard or comfort-height seats. We also check the flange height, the supply valve and the floor before we set it.

Replacing every toilet in a house is a good time to think about water use, because newer models use less water per flush. If you are updating a whole bathroom, our plumbing installation team coordinates the work.

What affects the cost of toilet repair and installation

  • Repair or replace. A flapper swap costs little. A new toilet adds the unit price.
  • Floor and flange condition. Rot or a damaged flange adds repair work.
  • Shutoff valve. An old valve that will not close may be replaced during the visit.
  • Specialty models. Wall-hung or macerating toilets involve more work.

Sudden overflow damage may fall under your homeowner's policy, while a worn part usually does not. Keep photos and receipts, and ask your insurer before repairs.

When it is not the toilet

A toilet that gurgles when another fixture drains points to a vent or a main line issue. If the whole house backs up, the cause is in the sewer line and sewer line cleaning is the right step. We find out which with a few questions on the phone.

Standards and sources for this job

Setting or replacing a toilet is plumbing work under the Illinois Plumbing Code. Illinois licenses plumbers through the Illinois Department of Public Health, and its verification tool lets you check a license before the visit.
