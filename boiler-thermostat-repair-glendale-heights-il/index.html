Boiler Thermostat Repair: Glendale Heights, IL Homeowner Guide

A boiler thermostat tells the boiler when to heat. When it fails, the house gets too cold, too hot or no heat at all, even though the boiler itself is fine. Check the batteries, the mode and the setpoint first. If those are right and the boiler does not respond, test the thermostat's wiring and the boiler's call-for-heat signal, or call a plumber.

This guide separates thermostat faults from boiler faults so you know where to look.

By the Glendale Heights Pro Plumbing team · Published 2026-11-16 · Updated 2026-11-16

Check the simple things

Start at the thermostat. Make sure it is set to heat and the temperature setting is above the room temperature. Replace the batteries if it is battery powered, even if the display is on, because weak batteries can show a display and still fail to send a signal.

If the display is blank on a wired unit, check the breaker for the boiler circuit. Smart thermostats add steps. A lost Wi-Fi connection, a paused schedule or an incorrect system type setting can stop a call for heat.

A quick test is to raise the setpoint and listen. If you hear a click and the boiler starts, the thermostat is working. If nothing happens, the problem is the thermostat, its wiring or the boiler's control circuit. A plumber can bridge the thermostat terminals to test the boiler directly.

Common thermostat failures

Old mechanical thermostats drift out of calibration or get dust on their contacts. Digital units fail from dead batteries, loose wires and failed sensors. A thermostat in the wrong place, such as above a vent or in direct sun, reads wrong and shuts the heat early. Wiring is a frequent culprit.

A loose wire at the thermostat or the boiler terminal interrupts the signal. If the thermostat was replaced recently, check that the wires went to the right terminals, because a hydronic system uses a simple two-wire circuit that is easy to mix up with a furnace setup.

What a repair or replacement involves

A plumber confirms the fault, then cleans, tightens or replaces parts. Replacing a thermostat is often simple on a basic two-wire system. A programmable or smart unit needs a compatible setup and correct programming. If the boiler has zone valves, each zone has its own thermostat, and a failed valve can look like a thermostat fault.

If the issue is the boiler's control board or a safety lockout, the repair is more involved, and our boiler repair page explains what we handle.

The Village of Glendale Heights' history page says the community has ranch homes, split-level homes, apartments and condominiums. The Village was organized in 1959, so the original building stock is more than 60 years old, and a boiler or thermostat of that age may still serve some houses.

The Illinois Department of Public Health licenses plumbers under the Illinois Plumbing License Law, and Village brochures state that plumbers working for hire must be registered and bonded with the Village.

Safety first

If you smell gas, see soot near the boiler or hear a carbon monoxide alarm, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911. Do not keep resetting a boiler that has locked out for a safety reason. A repeated lockout means a part or a condition needs attention.

Do not bypass a thermostat or a safety control to get heat. Those devices protect the house and the people in it.

Protect the house if heat is out

If the heat is out in winter, open the cabinets under sinks on exterior walls and let a faucet drip. Keep interior doors open to share warmth. A small electric heater placed safely can help for a short time. A house that freezes can lose pipes, and burst pipe repair becomes the next call.

Maintenance that helps

Replace thermostat batteries once a year. Dust the thermostat gently. Have the boiler serviced before each heating season. Bleed radiators that go cold at the top. These steps catch small problems before the first cold night.

When to call a plumber

Call if the thermostat is fine and the boiler does not respond, if the boiler locks out repeatedly, if radiators stay cold or if you see leaks, drips or rust. Water heater repair covers hot water problems when the boiler also supplies domestic hot water. Our team can sort which part is at fault.
