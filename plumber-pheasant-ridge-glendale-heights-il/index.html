Plumber in Pheasant Ridge and Glendale Terrace, Glendale Heights, IL

Pheasant Ridge and Glendale Terrace sit side by side north of Fullerton Avenue, and the two names are often used together. The area is mostly detached single-family houses. Homeowners here can phone and explain what is wrong, and a plumber can advise on the next step.

This page is about owning a whole house and the plumbing that comes with it. We cover what we could verify about the area and keep general advice clearly separate from local fact.

☎ (630) 822-9290

Two names for one stretch of town

Pheasant Ridge is the name on a village park and on a District 16 primary school. The village lists Pheasant Ridge Park at 0.87 acres with a basketball court, a play structure and a shelter. A real estate directory gives its address as 150 Placid Avenue.

Directories describe Pheasant Ridge as single-family houses built from 1970 to 1987, east of Schmale Road and north of Fullerton Avenue. They describe Glendale Terrace as single-family houses from the 1960s and 1970s, north of Fullerton Avenue and west of Bloomingdale Road.

One neighborhood data site groups them as a single area, "Glendale Terrace / Pheasant Ridge", and calls it mostly detached, owner-occupied homes. No official map was found, so these boundaries are approximate. By the same directories, Glendale Lakes lies across Fullerton Avenue to the south and has a different housing mix.

Owning the whole house means owning the whole list

With a detached house there is no association to call. The shutoff, the heater, the fixtures and the line to the street are all yours to look after. That makes a few habits worth having:

  • Know where the main shutoff is and exercise it once a year.
  • Read the age on the water heater label.
  • Keep notes on any repair, with dates and invoices.
  • Ask the village before work that may need a permit.

For anything in the street, the village's Water and Sewer Division is at 1615 Glen Ellyn Road. Permit questions go to Community Development. If the problem is outside your house, the water line repair and replacement page explains the buried service pipe.

Village landmarks that help place you

The village park list includes Pheasant Ridge Park, Ringneck Park at 1.2 acres and Glen Hill Park at 0.875 acres. These small neighborhood parks are useful reference points when you describe where you are. Americana Park, at 5.2 acres, has two tennis courts. When you call, give your street address, not just the neighborhood name.

Directories and residents draw these boundaries differently, and a street address removes the doubt.
