Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we handle when you visit this site or call our line.

☎ (630) 822-9290

Phone calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. We use what you tell the dispatcher, such as your name, address and plumbing problem, to schedule and perform the work you request. We do not sell the information you give us on a call.

This website

The site has no forms and does not ask you to type personal details. Like most sites, our hosting provider may log technical data such as your browser type and the pages you view. We use that information to keep the site running and secure.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to contact@proplumbingglendaleheights.com or the phone number on the contact page.
