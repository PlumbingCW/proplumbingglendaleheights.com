Boiler Repair in Glendale Heights, IL

A boiler that stops heating in a Glendale Heights winter is a serious problem, and our 24/7 line takes the call. Check that the boiler has power and the thermostat is calling for heat, then call so a plumber can diagnose it.

When a boiler fault needs a fast call

Cold weather turns a boiler problem into an urgent one. A house that cannot hold heat risks frozen pipes, and that adds to the repair bill. Call at the first sign of trouble, even if the house is still warm. Safety comes before comfort.

A gas smell, a yellow or flickering flame, soot around the unit or a carbon monoxide alarm means leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility. Do not relight a boiler that has shut off for a safety reason.

Hydronic heating is a closed loop of hot water moving through pipes or radiators. A loss of pressure often means a leak, and a pump that does not run leaves part of the house cold.

Boiler or water heater

A boiler heats water for the house's radiators or baseboards. A water heater heats the water you wash with. Some homes use a combination unit. If the problem is hot water only, water heater repair covers it. If the radiators are cold, a boiler technician starts with the pump, the thermostat and the pressure.

Older homes may have an older boiler. Age alone is not a reason to replace one, and a well-kept boiler can run for decades. We give you the repair and replacement numbers and let you decide.

Annual service and what it covers

A yearly check keeps a boiler safe and efficient. The technician checks combustion, the flue and the safeties, cleans the burner and tests pressure and the relief valve. It catches small faults before the first cold night. Scale and sediment lower efficiency over time.

Moderately hard water, which the DuPage Water Commission describes as about 8 grains per gallon, can leave deposits in a boiler's water side if the system takes on makeup water. A tight closed system takes on little, and a system that constantly refills has a leak that needs finding.

What affects the cost of boiler repair

  • Part and labor. A pump, a valve and a heat exchanger are very different repairs.
  • Boiler age and model. Older models may need parts that take time to source.
  • Leak location. A visible fitting is simple. A buried line is not.
  • Safety items. A failed flue or safety control must be fixed before the boiler runs.

Heating repair is not normally an insurance claim. Burst pipes from a heat failure and the resulting water damage may be covered, so photograph damage and call your insurer.

Protecting your pipes

If the boiler is down and the house is cooling, open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls and let a faucet drip. A line that freezes can split, and burst pipe repair becomes the next call. Keep the thermostat set above freezing even if you leave.

Homes in Glen Ellyn and Glendale Heights share the same winters, and older hydronic systems are common in both.

Permits and safety

Gas appliances and venting follow code, and the Village's Community Development office at (630) 260-6030 can confirm permits for a replacement. Plumbers working for hire must be registered and bonded with the Village.

Safety steps if the boiler stops heating

  • Smell gas, see soot or hear a carbon monoxide alarm: leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 first
  • Do not relight a boiler that shut off for a safety reason
  • Do not bypass a thermostat or a safety control to get heat
  • Keep the thermostat above freezing and open cabinet doors on exterior walls
  • Close the water feed and call if you see a leak or climbing pressure

Standards and sources for this job

Boiler work follows the manufacturer's instructions and the Illinois Plumbing Code, and gas and venting work must be done by qualified, licensed people. Illinois licenses plumbers under 225 ILCS 320, and the Village's brochures list Community Development at (630) 260-6030 for permits.
