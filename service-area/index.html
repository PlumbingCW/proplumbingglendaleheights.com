Service Area Around Glendale Heights, IL

Our dispatch is based in Glendale Heights and reaches five neighboring DuPage County towns. Each has its own page with local housing, water and ZIP details.

☎ (630) 822-9290

Five towns near our North Avenue base

BloomingdaleCarol StreamGlen EllynWheatonAddison

Distances below are estimates by road, not promises of arrival. Traffic on North Avenue, Army Trail Road and Roosevelt Road changes them hour to hour.

  • Bloomingdale, about 3 miles north-northwest, ZIP 60108
  • Carol Stream, about 4 miles west, ZIP 60188
  • Glen Ellyn, about 4 miles south-southeast, ZIP 60137
  • Wheaton, about 6 miles southwest, ZIPs 60187 and 60189
  • Addison, about 5 miles east, ZIP 60101

How dispatch works across the five towns

A plumber is sent from the nearest open position, not from a fixed route. Distances are estimates by road and the real time depends on traffic and open calls. The dispatcher gives you an honest window on the phone and tells you if a closer plumber frees up.

Every town page lists the ZIP codes we cover there, the water supply and a few facts about local housing. Use them to check that we serve your street, then call. If your address sits on a border between towns, call anyway, because ZIP codes do not follow village lines exactly.

Glendale Heights itself uses 60139 and is also a mailing name in 60108, 60137 and 60188.

What each town page tells you

  • Bloomingdale covers Old Town, the village trail system and a housing stock that mixes eras, with water supplied through the DuPage Water Commission.
  • Carol Stream covers a village platted from farmland in the late 1950s, with heavy warehouse and industrial space beside its homes.
  • Glen Ellyn covers an older village near the College of DuPage and the Metra line, where mixed pipe materials are common.
  • Wheaton covers the county seat and its two ZIP codes, and quotes the city's own water report.
  • Addison covers a village with a large industrial district, a creek and a mix of homes and businesses.

Reaching us from a town we have not listed

If you live in Lombard, Hanover Park, Roselle or another nearby community, call anyway. The dispatcher knows which plumbers are close and will tell you honestly whether we can reach your address the same day.

We only build a page for a town once we have real facts about its water, housing and ZIP codes, so a missing page does not mean a missing service. Whatever the town, the first step does not change. Close the main shutoff, stop using drains if sewage is backing up and call (630) 822-9290.

The line is answered 24/7, and a plumber is dispatched the same day when one is open.

Small places we also reach

Wood Dale and Itasca sit north of Addison and are covered through that page. Winfield and Warrenville sit west and southwest of Wheaton and are covered through the Wheaton page. Woodridge and Schaumburg are farther out, so call and ask whether a plumber can reach you. You can also review all plumbing services and the company details.

Our service area around Glendale Heights

Dispatch covers Glendale Heights plus Bloomingdale, Carol Stream, Glen Ellyn, Wheaton and Addison, with the phone line open around the clock.
