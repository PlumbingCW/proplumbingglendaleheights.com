Kitchen Sink Not Draining but Not Clogged: Glendale Heights, IL

A kitchen sink that will not drain even though the trap is clear has a problem farther down the line. The usual causes are a clog beyond the trap, a blocked vent that slows the flow, a disposal or dishwasher connection holding water or a partial blockage in the main line. Work through them in order, and call a plumber if the water still sits.

This guide gives the order to check and explains what each result means.

By the Glendale Heights Pro Plumbing team · Published 2026-11-16 · Updated 2026-11-16

Start with what else is slow

Run water in the bathroom sink, the tub and the washer drain. If only the kitchen sink is slow, the problem is in the kitchen branch. If other fixtures are slow too, the main line is the likely cause. If a toilet gurgles when the kitchen sink drains, the vent or the main is involved.

This test saves time. Many people keep working on the kitchen trap when the real problem is a partial blockage in the main.

The branch line beyond the trap

After the trap, the pipe runs to a tee in the wall or floor and joins the larger drain. Grease and food waste often build up here. A hand auger run through the trap arm reaches this section. If the auger finds the clog, the sink should drain at once.

Grease coats the pipe, so the clog may come back unless the line is cleaned thoroughly. If the auger does not clear it, stop. Forcing a cable into a pipe that is cracked or collapsed can damage it, and a plumber can look with a camera first.

Vent problems

Drain pipes need air. A vent pipe through the roof lets air in behind the water, so water flows smoothly. A blocked vent causes slow draining, gurgling and sometimes sewer smells. Leaves, nests and ice can block a vent.

A plumber can check the vent from the roof and clear it, and it is not a safe job for a ladder at home in winter. Slow draining combined with gurgling in a different fixture is a classic vent sign.

The disposal and dishwasher

A disposal that is jammed or partly clogged at its outlet holds water above the blades. A dishwasher drain hose that connects to the disposal can back up into the sink if the disposal outlet is blocked.

Check that the knockout plug in the dishwasher inlet was removed when the disposal was installed, because a plug left in place stops the dishwasher from draining at all. Our garbage disposal team can free a jam and check the outlet.

Plumbing code and permits to know before drain work

The Illinois Department of Public Health lists the Illinois Plumbing Code under Title 77 of the Illinois Administrative Code on its plumbing page, and it licenses plumbers under the Illinois Plumbing License Law. Any new drain, vent or fixture work in your kitchen should be built to that code by a licensed plumber.

The Village of Glendale Heights publishes brochures that direct residents to Community Development at (630) 260-6030 for permit questions, and its water heater brochure says plumbers doing contracted installations must be registered and bonded with the Village. A simple unclogging does not need a permit, but if you add a drain or relocate a sink, call first.

The main line

If the kitchen sink and other fixtures all drain slowly, the main line is partly blocked. Roots, grease and buildup in the main slow everything. If your house has an outside cleanout, remove the cap carefully and see whether water stands there.

Standing water means the blockage is farther down, and a plumber with a drum machine or a jetter can clear it. This is the point to book a visit. Sewer line cleaning clears the main, and hydro jetting is the better tool when grease has coated the pipe.

What not to do

Do not pour chemical drain cleaner into a sink that holds standing water. It sits there and will burn skin when the trap is opened later. Do not use a plunger on a double sink without blocking the other drain. Do not run the dishwasher until the sink drains. Do not keep adding water to a slow drain.

If it backs up into the sink, stop.

Prevention

Scrape plates, use a strainer and never pour grease into the sink. Flush the drain with hot water after washing greasy dishes. Have an older drain cleaned if it clogs more than once a year. These habits cost little and prevent the same visit in a few months.
