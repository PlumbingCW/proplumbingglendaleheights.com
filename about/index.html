About Glendale Heights Pro Plumbing

Glendale Heights Pro Plumbing is a 24/7 emergency and repair line for Glendale Heights, Illinois and the DuPage County towns around it.

☎ (630) 822-9290

What we do

We take the call, learn what is happening in the house and send a licensed plumber to fix it. The work covers the problems this part of DuPage County sees most, including drain cleaning, water heater replacement, leak detection, sewer line repair and sump pump repair. There are no web forms on this site.

Every request starts with a phone call to (630) 822-9290, because a plumbing emergency needs a conversation, not a ticket number.

How the village sets the rules

The Village of Glendale Heights was organized in July 1959 and was first called Glendale, then renamed in March 1960 to avoid confusion with another Glendale in southern Illinois. The name reflects its place between Glen Ellyn and Bloomingdale.

The village government publishes its own plumbing guidance through the Department of Community Development, and that guidance shapes how we work. Two examples matter most to homeowners. The village states that every water heater installation needs a permit, and it states that plumbers working for hire must be registered and bonded with the Village.

We follow both rules and tell you when a permit is part of the job.

How Illinois licenses plumbers

Illinois licenses plumbers, plumbing contractors and apprentice plumbers through the Illinois Department of Public Health under the Illinois Plumbing License Law. A plumber who does work for another person is a plumbing contractor, and must register with the state, pay an annual fee and carry liability and workers' compensation coverage. IDPH offers an online license verification tool.

We encourage you to use it with any plumber, including ours. You can also read more about our emergency plumbing service and which services we dispatch for.

How a call works

You call (630) 822-9290 and a dispatcher answers. You describe what is happening, give the street address and say whether the main shutoff is closed. The dispatcher books the next open plumber and tells you what to do while you wait, then the plumber arrives, diagnoses the problem and quotes the work before starting.

That order never changes, whether the call comes at noon or at 2 a.m. The first step is always to stop the water, and the second is to get a person on the line.

Where we work

The office address is 739 North Ave in Glendale Heights, ZIP 60139. From there we dispatch across DuPage County to Bloomingdale, Carol Stream, Glen Ellyn, Wheaton and Addison. Each town has its own page with the local water supply, housing age and ZIP codes.

The standards we hold ourselves to

Every plumber who goes to a Glendale Heights home is expected to explain the problem in plain words before touching a pipe. They show the failed part where that is possible, name the options and give a price. If a repair will not last, the plumber says so and prices the replacement next to it.

We also keep the permit question honest. A water heater swap needs a Village permit, a new sump pit needs one and some drain work may too. We tell you when a permit applies and who pulls it, so nothing is left to surprise you at resale or in an insurance claim.

What we will not do

We do not publish invented reviews, star ratings or made-up customer counts, and we do not quote a price before a plumber has seen the problem. If a job needs a permit, we say so. If a repair is not worth the money, we say that too.

Need a plumber now? Call the 24/7 line and a dispatcher will help.
