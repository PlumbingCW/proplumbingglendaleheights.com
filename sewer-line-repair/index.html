Emergency Sewer Line Repair in Glendale Heights, IL

Sewage coming up in a drain is a health risk and an emergency, and our 24/7 line is open for it. Stop using water, keep people and pets away from the spill and call so a plumber can find the break.

Sewer backup: what to do first

A sewer backup means the line cannot carry the flow. Stop using water throughout the house, because everything you drain adds to the problem. Open windows if there is an odor, and keep the area clear until it is cleaned. Call us and describe where the backup is.

The lowest drain, usually a basement floor drain, shows trouble first. The plumber runs a camera, finds the break or blockage and explains the options. Same-day service is available when a plumber is open, day or night.

Older homes built from the late 1950s onward may have clay or cast iron drain pipe. These materials can crack, separate at joints and admit roots. The material in your own line is something the camera answers, so we do not guess.

Repair options

Open-cut repair digs a trench to the pipe and replaces the damaged section. It works well for short breaks and for pipe that has deteriorated badly. Trenchless methods, such as pipe lining or pipe bursting, can repair or replace a line with far less digging, and they preserve landscaping and driveways. Lining is not available for every pipe.

A collapsed or heavily offset section may need excavation first. We show you the camera footage and explain which option fits, and the choice is yours.

Finding the problem first

No repair starts without a camera look. We feed a camera down the line, record what we see and mark the location of any break on the surface. If the main also needs cleaning first, sewer line cleaning clears it before the camera goes in.

A separate sewer inspection visit is useful before buying a home or when you want a baseline.

Permits and the village

Work on the sewer lateral may involve the Village. The Public Works department can confirm the current rules for your address at (630) 260-6040, and we help you ask the right question.

If a sump pump is tied to the sanitary line, that must be corrected, because the Village states a sump pump may not discharge into the sanitary sewer.

What affects the cost of sewer line repair

  • Length and depth of the damage. A short, shallow section costs less than a long, deep one.
  • Method. Spot repair, lining and full replacement are priced differently.
  • Surface restoration. Lawn, landscaping and hard surfaces affect the final bill.
  • Permits and utility locates. These are handled as part of the planning.

Sewer lateral damage from age is often not covered by homeowner's policies, but sewage backup damage inside the house may be, with the right endorsement. Ask your insurer before work begins.

Protecting the line afterward

Tree roots follow moisture. Keeping large trees away from the line helps, and so does a periodic camera check on an older line. Do not flush wipes, and do not pour grease down the kitchen drain. If the backup is mostly grease, hydro jetting may clear the line without a repair.

Glen Ellyn and other nearby older communities call us for the same repairs.

Standards and sources for this job

Sewer lateral repair is covered by the Illinois Plumbing Code and, depending on the work, by Village permit rules. The Village's sump pump brochure cites Section 7-5-2 of the Village code, which bars a sump pump from discharging into a sanitary sewer, and that matters when a line overloads in heavy rain.
