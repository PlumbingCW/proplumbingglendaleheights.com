Contact Glendale Heights Pro Plumbing

The fastest way to reach a plumber is to call (630) 822-9290. The line is answered 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays.

☎ (630) 822-9290

Call, do not wait for a form

This site has no contact form, because water on the floor cannot wait for an email reply. Call and speak to a dispatcher. If you reach voicemail during a busy storm, leave your name, your street address and what is happening, and the call will be returned.

For problems that are not urgent, such as a faucet drip or a slow drain, the same number books a regular appointment. You can also email contact@proplumbingglendaleheights.com for billing or general questions, though the phone is always faster for repairs.

What to tell the dispatcher

  • Your street address and nearest cross street, so the plumber can find you in the dark
  • Where the water or sewage is, and whether it is still flowing
  • Whether you have already closed the main shutoff valve
  • The age of the water heater or fixture involved, if you know it
  • Whether anyone in the home is elderly, ill or has no other water source

Where we are

Our address is 739 North Ave, Glendale Heights, IL 60139. North Avenue is Illinois Route 64, the main east-west commercial corridor through the village. The phone line is the way to book service, so please call before you drive over.
