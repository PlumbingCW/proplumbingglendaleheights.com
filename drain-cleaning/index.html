Emergency Drain Cleaning in Glendale Heights, IL

A drain that will not clear is urgent when it backs up into the house, and our 24/7 line answers nights and weekends. Stop running water in the home, because every gallon you add has nowhere to go, and call for a plumber.

One slow drain or the whole house?

A single slow sink or tub is usually a local clog in that fixture's trap or branch line. It is a good job for snaking and often a same-day fix. The picture changes when more than one fixture slows, the toilet gurgles when a sink drains or the lowest drain in the house backs up.

That pattern points to the main line. A blocked main can push sewage out of a basement floor drain, and that is a true emergency. Tell the dispatcher which fixtures are affected, because it decides what the plumber brings.

Drain backup: what to do first

Stop using water everywhere in the house. If sewage has reached the floor, keep children and pets out of the area and do not run a wet vacuum on it. Turn off a water heater or furnace only if it is standing in water and you can do it safely.

Call us, describe the symptoms and we dispatch a plumber the same day when one is available. Day or night, the line is open. Do not pour chemical drain openers into a standing backup. They rarely clear a deep blockage, and they can burn the person who then has to open the trap.

What clogs drains in Glendale Heights homes

Kitchen lines carry grease and food waste. A kitchen sink that is slow but the dishwasher drains fine points to the kitchen branch. Bathroom lines collect hair and soap. A tub that drains slowly after a long shower is a hair and soap case far more often than a main line case. Older homes add another cause.

Many houses built from the late 1950s have long runs of older drain pipe, and tree roots look for joints. If your lowest drain slows after heavy rain, ask us about roots or a damaged section, which belongs on the sewer line repair page.

Clogs we see on call

The toilet that will not flush is a common emergency, and a plunger is the right first step. If water rises in the tub when you flush, stop and call. A flushed wipe labeled "flushable" is a repeat cause.

The household disposal fails the same way, so a stuck unit may be a garbage disposal job, not a drain job.

What affects the cost of drain cleaning

  • Location of the clog. A fixture trap costs less than a clog deep in the main.
  • Method needed. A hand snake, a drum machine and a jetting visit are different jobs.
  • Access. An open cleanout is quick. Pulling a toilet to reach the line adds time.
  • Camera use. A camera visit adds a step, and it also tells you what is wrong.

Drain cleaning is normally maintenance and not an insurance claim. If sewage damaged your finished basement, ask your insurer about backup coverage, since it is often an add-on.

Preventing the next clog

Use strainers in tubs and kitchen sinks, and never pour grease down a drain. Run hot water after washing dishes. Keep wipes, paper towels and feminine products out of the toilet. A yearly cleaning of a drain with a history makes sense, but it is not required for every house.

Nearby homeowners in Glen Ellyn call us for the same causes, and many have the same older drain lines.

Standards and sources for this job

Drain work that opens or alters piping falls under the Illinois Plumbing Code in Title 77 of the Illinois Administrative Code, and a licensed plumber is the right person to do it. Plain cleaning of a clog does not normally need a permit, and Community Development at (630) 260-6030 can confirm for larger jobs.
