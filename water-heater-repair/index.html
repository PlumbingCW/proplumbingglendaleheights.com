When water heater trouble is an emergency

A heater that is dripping from the tank body, from a rusted seam or from the bottom is usually past repair. A heater whose relief valve is blowing hot water is a safety problem. A heater with no hot water at all is urgent for a household with children or an elderly resident.

If you smell gas near a gas heater, leave the home and call your gas utility or 911 before you call us. Do not turn switches or flame sources on or off while you are inside. Everything else is a repair visit. Call at any hour, describe what you see and the dispatcher books the next open plumber.

Why the water here matters to your heater

Glendale Heights gets Lake Michigan water through the DuPage Water Commission. The Commission describes it as about 8 grains per gallon, or roughly 137 mg/L as calcium carbonate, which is moderately hard. Over years, that leaves scale on heating elements and in the bottom of a tank.

That scale is behind much of the popping, the slow recovery and the shortened life you see in tanks past ten years. A yearly flush helps, and checking the anode rod tells us how much protective metal remains. If the hardness bothers you, a softener reduces the scale load.

How repair compares with replacing

A heater under about eight years with a failed thermostat, element or thermocouple is usually worth repairing. A heater past a decade that leaks, rusts at the bottom or needs a second repair in a year usually costs more to keep than to replace. We give you that comparison honestly and let you decide.

Age is printed on the tank label in the serial number. Send us the brand and the model if you have it, and we can tell you quickly which side of the line you are on.

What affects the cost of water heater repair

  • The part. A thermocouple, a thermostat and a gas valve are priced very differently.
  • Fuel type. Gas and electric heaters fail in different places.
  • Age and brand. Parts for older models can be harder to find.
  • Venting and code issues. Some repairs reveal an old vent or a missing expansion tank that must be corrected.

Homeowner's insurance usually does not pay for the heater itself. It may pay for water damage when a tank bursts suddenly. Photograph damage and call your insurer.

Permits and the village

Glendale Heights states that every water heater installation needs a permit, whether a plumber or the homeowner does the work. A like-for-like repair does not normally need one, but a heater swap does. We tell you which one applies before work starts. You can confirm by calling the Village's Community Development office at (630) 260-6030.

Tankless heaters

Tankless units fail differently. They throw error codes, scale up inside the heat exchanger and lose flow when an inlet screen clogs. We repair and flush them on the tankless water heater page. Call us with the model and the code on the display.

Standards and sources for this job

The Village of Glendale Heights publishes a water heater brochure covering permits, venting and the temperature and pressure relief valve. It says contracted installations require a plumber who is registered and bonded with the Village, and it refers to older code editions, so confirm current rules with Community Development.
